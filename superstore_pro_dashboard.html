3 strategic recommendations
Fix discounting
Any discount above 20% generates losses. Cap discounts or reprice — this single change could recover $135K in lost profit annually.
Rethink Furniture
Tables and Bookcases are destroying value. Either raise prices, reduce promotions, or discontinue the lowest-margin SKUs in these sub-categories.
Double down on Tech
Technology delivers 17.4% margin with no structural losses. Copiers alone beat all of Furniture. Prioritise this category in marketing and inventory.
